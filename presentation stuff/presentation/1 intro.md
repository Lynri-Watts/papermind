一、双语讲稿（可参考）

请想象这样一个场景。凌晨三点，你已经连续读了六个小时的论文，眼睛酸涩刺痛。终于找到一篇看起来跟你的研究方向完美契合的论文——等等。推荐这篇论文的AI工具呢？它刚刚编造了作者的名字。期刊也不存在。你白白浪费了两个小时，追查了一条根本不存在的参考文献。

Imagine this. It's 3 AM. You've been reading papers for six hours. Your eyes are burning. You finally find a paper that seems perfect for your research — but wait. The AI tool that recommended it? It just made up the author's name. And the journal doesn't exist. You've just wasted two hours chasing a ghost citation.

熟悉吗？Sound familiar?

来看看现实的数据。每年，超过300万篇学术论文发表。换算一下，大约每10秒钟就有一篇新论文诞生。即使是最勤奋的研究者，也只能读完自己领域相关文献的极小一部分。我们淹没在信息的海洋里，却在饥渴地寻找知识。

Here's the reality. Every year, over 3 million academic papers are published. That's roughly one paper every 10 seconds. Even the most dedicated researcher can only read a tiny fraction of what's relevant to their field. We're drowning in information, starving for knowledge.

再看看我们今天使用的工具。ChatGPT、Gemini、Deepseek——确实强大。但它们把科研当成了一台自动售货机：你投进去一个问题，它吐出一个答案。没有对话。没有协作。不会记住你之前发现了什么。最致命的是——它们会说谎。研究表明，在学术场景下，AI模型编造参考文献的概率高达30%。每一句输出都必须人工核实。这不是效率的提升，这是一种新的劳动负担。

Now look at the tools we have today. ChatGPT, Gemini, Deepseek — powerful, yes. But they treat research like a vending machine: you insert a question, out comes an answer. There's no conversation. No collaboration. No memory of what you've already discovered. And worst of all — they lie. Studies show that AI models fabricate references up to 30% of the time in academic contexts. Every single output must be manually verified. That's not efficiency. That's a new form of labor.

我们相信，一定有更好的方式。We believe there's a better way.

如果AI助手不只是回答问题，而是与你并肩工作呢？如果它就住在你的浏览器里，理解你的研究方向，像GitHub Copilot帮助程序员写代码一样，帮你穿行在文献的海洋中——不是替代你的思考，而是放大你的思考能力？

What if your AI assistant didn't just answer questions, but worked alongside you? What if it lived inside your browser, understood your research direction, and helped you navigate the sea of literature the same way GitHub Copilot helps developers write code — not replacing your thinking, but amplifying it?

这正是我们打造的产品。That's exactly what we built.

我和我的团队一起创造了 PaperMind —— 全球首个浏览器原生的AI科研副驾驶。

Our team have created PaperMind — the world's first browser-native AI research co-pilot.
